<h2>Arcane Towers 0.0.0-beta.1</h2>

<p>First beta: four arcane tower dungeons.</p>

<h3>Added</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Four towers</strong>, one per biome family: Tower of Arcane Stone (plains/forests, Tauren guardian), Frost Tower (snowy lands, Goliath), Sandstone Tower (deserts/badlands/savannas, Centaur) and Umbral Tower (dark forests/swamps, Mountain Giant).</li>
<li>Climb each tower floor by floor around its sealed shaft, jump in from the last floor and land in the dungeon below.</li>
<li><strong>Guardians</strong> awaken once in the deepest hall of each tower, stronger than their wild kin.</li>
<li><strong>Exit Stone</strong>: breaks the tower's seal and takes you back to the surface, in front of the tower door.</li>
<li>Towers are <strong>sealed</strong> &mdash; no mining, building, fluids or explosions inside &mdash; until their Exit Stone is used.</li>
<li>Floor and vault loot, English and Spanish.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Known issues</h3>
<ul>
<li>Dungeons can be very large and full of spawners. A smaller, better-paced layout is in the works.</li>
<li>Occasionally a dungeon generates without its final hall; that tower then unseals itself when you enter, so you are never trapped.</li>
</ul>
