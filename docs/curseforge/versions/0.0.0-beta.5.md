<h2>Arcane Towers 0.0.0-beta.5 &mdash; Looks and decoration</h2>

<h3>Changed</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Magical exteriors</strong>: every tower now has its own striking silhouette &mdash; copper spires and amethyst runes on the Stone Tower, a tall frozen spire with floating ice rings on the Frost Tower, a golden observatory dome on the Sandstone Tower, and a leaning, broken crown with a floating ring on the Umbral Tower.</li>
<li><strong>Decorated interiors</strong>: libraries, alchemy labs, scriptoria and astronomers' studies on every floor.</li>
<li><strong>Irregular dungeon rooms</strong> with pillars, collapsed corners and rubble.</li>
<li>The Frost Tower's Exit Stone now stands on a dais in the middle of the hall.</li>
</ul>

<p>Requires Majestic Bestiary 0.0.0-beta.5 (new creature models and minimap icons).</p>
